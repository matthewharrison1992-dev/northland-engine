## Rules learned in v1 (all still apply; most also in the daily-task rules and /areas/fantasy-brief-lessons.md)
1. Scoreboard lags a day; add missing days from box scores and say so.
2. Calibrate: weight questionables; any category within ~3% is a coin flip; early-season samples overstate hot and cold teams.
3. Name players with full name and NBA team.
4. Check the schedule before flagging a bench problem.
5. Judge weeks by all-play as well as the result.
6. Judge drops by category shape (the Week 1 big-man drops turned REB from a strength into a weakness).
7. Adds that fill empty slots during injuries are worth far more than marginal swaps.
8. Re-test default drops with live data at the time.
9. Don't chase blocks on waivers; blocks need a trade.
